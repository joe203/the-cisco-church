# Photo Shot List — TheCiscoChurch.org

This is a small site, so it needs very few photos. **Four good ones will carry
the whole thing.** Landscape (wide) orientation, bright, real, candid. Phone
photos are fine — higher resolution is better since we can always crop down.

Drop files in `public/images/` and we'll place them.

---

## The four that matter

**1. Congregation in worship — the hero.**
Wide shot from the back or side. Warm, full of people. Lots of horizontal room,
because text sits over the left side.

**2. Fellowship candid.**
People talking, laughing, sharing a meal or coffee. This is the photo that
answers *"would I fit in here?"* — more important than it looks.

**3. Someone preaching or teaching.**
Used alongside the sermon sections. A shot from the back of the room including
part of the congregation works better than a tight shot of the pulpit.

**4. Exterior of the building.**
Clean shot of the building and sign, ideally in good afternoon or evening light.
This helps first-time visitors recognize the place when they pull up.

---

## Nice to have, not needed for launch
- A wide shot of the empty auditorium — useful as a quiet background panel.
- Anything with genuine Cisco character: the sign, the parking lot at dusk, the
  street the building sits on.

---

## Please avoid
- Stock photos of hands raised or diverse strangers in a boardroom.
- Heavily filtered or over-edited images.
- Portrait-orientation phone photos for the hero — they crop badly.
